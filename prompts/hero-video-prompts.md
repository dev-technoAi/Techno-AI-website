# TechnoAi hero video — image + video prompts (real shoot style)

Goal: one 18-second looping hero video (6 shots × 3 s), one shot per TechnoAi solution.
Look: **like a real corporate documentary shoot**, not CGI — real locations, real people at work,
natural/practical light, a real camera on a gimbal or shoulder, slight film grain, real wear and
dust on equipment. Evening / dusk / indoor low light, so the footage is naturally dark.

Why the earlier prompts felt "AI": glowing holograms, hex colour codes and words like "cinematic,
premium, glowing" push the generators into a CGI/advert look. These prompts avoid all of that.
Brand colour (#0f1a1b + #146b8c) is added **afterwards** in the colour grade, and any line-graphic
overlays (TII style) are added on the website/in editing — not generated.

Workflow
1. ChatGPT: one image per shot, 16:9 landscape. Paste the image prompt as it is.
2. Google Flow: use the image as the **start frame**, paste the matching video prompt,
   16:9, 1080p, no audio. Longer than 3 s is fine — I trim.
3. Send me `shot-1.mp4` … `shot-6.mp4` (+ the images). I cut, colour-match to the brand,
   add soft transitions, make the loop seamless and put it in the hero.

Keep the main subject on the **right two-thirds**; the left side stays calmer for the headline.

## Shot 1 — AI & Robotics (0–3 s)

**Image prompt (ChatGPT)**
```
Documentary photograph from a real corporate video shoot, 16:9 landscape. Inside a working robotics lab in the evening, an engineer in a dark polo shirt stands at a workbench calibrating a white collaborative robot arm with a laptop beside it. Real lab details: cable trays, tool cases, a few stickers on equipment, fingerprints on the metal, slightly cluttered bench. Light comes from overhead practical LED panels and the laptop screen, the rest of the room falls into shadow. Shot on an ARRI Alexa Mini with a 35mm lens at eye level, natural colours, soft contrast, light film grain, true-to-life skin tones. Subject on the right two-thirds of the frame, left side darker. Not CGI, not 3D render, not an advert, no glowing effects, no text, no logos.
```

**Video prompt (Google Flow, start frame = shot-1 image)**
```
Real documentary footage. The engineer taps the laptop and the robot arm makes one slow, precise movement, rotating at the base and lowering its gripper. The camera moves slowly forward on a gimbal with natural, slightly imperfect motion. Ordinary lab ambience, practical lights, no effects, no holograms, no text. Natural colours, light film grain, no music.
```

## If it still looks "AI" — add these to the prompt

- Too clean / plastic → `real imperfections: scratches, dust, cable mess, uneven lighting, slightly imperfect framing`
- Too dramatic / advert look → `plain natural lighting like a real documentary, low contrast, no lens flares, no rim light`
- Faces look fake → show people **from behind or over the shoulder**, or only their hands
- Too bright → `evening, low light, deep shadows, only practical lights`
- Text or logos appear → `no readable text, no letters, no logos anywhere`
- Want UAE feel → add `Emirati engineer in a white kandura` or `Emirati professional in a black abaya`
